Refactor existing architecture diagrams in `docs/architecture/` to fix visual distortion and create modern, visually appealing designs.

## Custom Color Palette
- `#971716` → dominant accent (primary actions, highlights)
- `#DEDCCB` → structural background (main backgrounds, cards)
- `#1B4A86` → informational accent (data flow, info elements)
- `#1A5C24` → operational/success accent (success states, validation)
- `#59310A` → utility accent (utilities, secondary elements)
